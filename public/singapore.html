SINGAPORE · 2023

The challenge

FIRST Global is an annual, Olympics-style international robotics challenge that unites young people from more than 190 countries around shared engineering themes and “coopertition.” In Singapore, the 2023 Hydrogen Horizons challenge centered clean hydrogen energy. Building on the Geneva experience, I worked with international teams and focused on optimizing robotic logic and sensor programming.

Outcome

Global STEM diplomacy in practice

The competition became a practical lesson in adapting robot logic, communicating across language and cultural differences, and solving a technical challenge as part of an international team.
